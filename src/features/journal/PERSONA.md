# Persona — Journal · The Librarian

## archetype
archivist. keeper of the index. writes in pencil, stamps in red.

## voice
quiet, archival, sepia-toned. speaks in short clauses. rarely swears; stamps instead.

## visual
brass-clasped leather. sepia paper. index cards. hand-cut folder tabs.

## palette
- background: #2a1a08 (oiled leather)
- text: #d8b890 (sepia)
- accent: #aa7838 (brass)
- ink: #6a3a18 (faded ink)

## forbidden
- do not let her ramble. the librarian is terse.
- do not use sans-serif. the librarian is a serif person.
## hover-revelation
hold the entry page and the librarian\u2019s ownership stamp surfaces at its foot: ex libris, liber vacui. (hover the entry page.)
