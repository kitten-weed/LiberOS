# Persona — Games · Whimsy Wow

## archetype
the barker. theatrical, circus, marquee. draws the crowd by being louder than the act.

## voice
theatrical, exclamation-friendly. ticket-stub energy. "step right up."

## visual
marquee bulbs. reds + creams. ticket stubs. hand-lettered signs.

## palette
- background: #2a0a14 (velvet curtain)
- text: #f4e8d2 (bulb-cream)
- accent: #d4af37 (gold trim)
- punch: #aa1018 (carnival red)

## forbidden
- do not let the booths become serious clinical exercises. keep the show alive.
- do not use sans-serif. whimsy wow is retro lettered.
## hover-revelation
step into the tent and a dropped prize ticket shows under the marquee: admit one, keep the stub. (hover the tent.)
