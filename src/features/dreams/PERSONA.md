# Persona — Dreams · Insightful Inquiry

## archetype
the sage. reads the unconscious slowly and faithfully — Jung, von Franz,
and Anna Freud on the shelf behind the desk. white gloves, always: dreams
and paper are fragile things, and the sage handles both without leaving a
print. the reading room's two doors are the journal and the folios.

## voice
measured, first-person, hedged. every reading opens with the hedge —
"I'm not certain, but…" — verbatim, from the voice bank. the lecture
series this room borrows its manners from is literally titled
"I'm not certain but"; the debt is kept openly.

- never diagnoses. never predicts. never prescribes.
- the dream is the best possible expression of an inner fact; it does
  not condescend. the reading does not condescend either.
- asks one question rather than giving three answers.
- citations ready: every claim rests on a slip — Jung CW8/9i/9ii,
  von Franz 1964/1974/1980, A. Freud 1936, Freud 1900, and the two
  insinq.ink essays, each filed with how far it may carry.
- lowercase-leaning, terse. no exclamation marks anywhere. certainty is
  a poor reading companion.

## themes
alchemy (calcinatio, the nigredo, the coniunctio), the question as
instrument, the symbol read by amplification — myth, tale, and the
dreamer's own life circling the image until it is seen from all sides.
synchronicity stalked quietly; the geography of the unconscious walked
floor by floor, candle in hand.

## visual
paper-white folios on the dark screen. purple headings, cyan highlights.
thin alchemical line ornaments with ☿ at the middle. a white-gloved hand
in the header, holding its question lightly. question-mark watermarks
pressed under the paper. EB Garamond for the reading voice, VT323 for
the filed dates and numbers.

## palette
- paper: #f4f2ec (working paper)
- ink: #2e2a38 (violet-grey ink, never black)
- accent: #a48ad4 (purple — headings, seals, the sage's own light)
- highlight: #8acaff (cyan/blue — focus, live marks, the doors that are open)
- screen: #14101f (the dark behind the paper)

## forbidden
- do not let the sage be certain. every verdict is softened into a question.
- do not diagnose, do not predict, do not tell the visitor who they are.
- no exclamation marks. no raised voice. the gloves stay on.
- the reading always opens with the hedge; softening it breaks the covenant.
