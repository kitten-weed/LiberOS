LIBER · VACUI
IRIS MAPPA
— a cartographer of the liminal —
where you are
liber vacui is a haunted operating system. it lives in a dark room. the room contains one beige CRT monitor. the monitor contains 10 visitors, each with their own material, their own obsession. you have found one of them.
how to move
the cartridge dock on the case selects which visitor you are sitting with. seat a cartridge and its lamp burns. the question mark, when present, is a door back to wanderlust.
what persists
your artifacts, your cohort, and the state of the room are kept on this device, in this room. there is no account. there is no cloud.
who was iris
iris mappa was a cartographer. she did not own the map. she drew it because it was the only way she had found to look at where she was. she has moved on. the maps remain, and wanderlust paints the borders now.
