Hi there!

My name is RAT, and this is my project. Before you begin there are a few honest things first, for safety.

This is an art therapy tool! Like a workbook you might pick up in a self help section. I built this from real research; I'm a student, not your therapist. You should not use it as a replacement for therapy, and if you are in crisis you should call a crisis line, not use this tool for any reason.

Crisis lines, if you need one right now:
US — call or text 988
Canada — call or text 988
UK & Ireland — Samaritans, 116 123
Anywhere else — search “helpline” plus your town.

If things get heavy inside the tool, three places are built for it: the games room’s quiet floor teaches a distress-tolerance skill set, the sea lets you give something heavy to water that takes without asking, and the dream lens keeps readings honest — one lens, never a verdict.

This tool is based on IFS and “Amplification,” a concept in analytical psychology. It can make things worse if you are carrying immediate, unprocessed trauma or have C-PTSD and exploring other “parts” of the identity could be harmful.
